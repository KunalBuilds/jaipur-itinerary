Practical Tips

Important things to know before you go.

01

Start Early

For forts, early morning is much better than midday. Heat and crowds increase significantly.

02

Don't Overpack Days

Don't try to visit all monuments in one or two days. You'll spend too much time in traffic.

03

Stay Flexible

Rain, traffic and fatigue can easily change your schedule. Keep one flexible block every day.

04

Carry Water

Especially on fort days. Carry 1–2 litres and refill whenever possible. Stay hydrated.

05

Check Timings

Official timings can change because of festivals, maintenance, weather or special events.

06

Extra Departure Time

Jaipur traffic can make short journeys considerably longer. Add 30–60 min buffer.
