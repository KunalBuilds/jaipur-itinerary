Important Shopping Rule

Do not buy expensive jewellery, gemstones, or precious items simply because a seller says they are "government approved" or "export quality."

  1. Compare multiple shops
  2. Ask for a detailed bill
  3. Check certification
  4. Avoid pressure sales
  5. Do not assume a tourist-shop recommendation means the price is fair

Getting Around Jaipur

Transport options for comfortable sightseeing.

local_taxi

Cab + Driver

Best for full sightseeing days. Recommended for Day 2, Day 3, and Day 5 day trips.

electric_rickshaw

Auto / E-rickshaw

Useful for short distances around the old city. Confirm fare before starting if not metered.

directions_walk

Walking

Best around Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Johari & Bapu Bazaar. Don't try to walk between every attraction.
