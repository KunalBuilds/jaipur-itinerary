# Jaipur 5-Day Travel Plan

**Trip length:** 5 days / 4 nights  
**Destination:** Jaipur, Rajasthan, India  
**Style:** Comfortable, balanced sightseeing + food + shopping  
**Best suited for:** First-time visitors

> **Important:** This plan assumes 5 full travel days. If your arrival is late on Day 1 or departure is early on Day 5, shift the activities accordingly.

## Quick Overview

| Day | Main Area | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Pink City | Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Johari Bazaar |
| Day 2 | Amer | Amer Fort, Panna Meena ka Kund, Jaigarh Fort, Jal Mahal |
| Day 3 | Nahargarh + Old Jaipur | Nahargarh Fort, Gaitore, Albert Hall, Birla Mandir |
| Day 4 | Culture + Shopping | Patrika Gate, museums/markets, local food, shopping |
| Day 5 | Flexible / Day Trip | Jaipur shopping + optional Abhaneri or Pushkar |

---

# Before You Go

## When to Visit

Rajasthan Tourism lists **July–September** as the monsoon period, with typical temperatures around **21–35°C** and rainfall around **100–165 mm**. October–December is considerably drier, while January–March is generally cooler.

If you are travelling during August, keep some flexibility for rain and avoid scheduling every outdoor attraction back-to-back.

## Where to Stay

For a first Jaipur trip, consider:

- **Bani Park:** good overall base, convenient for transport and restaurants.
- **MI Road:** central and convenient for first-time visitors.
- **C-Scheme:** better for restaurants, cafés and a more modern atmosphere.
- **Pink City:** best if you want to stay close to the old city's markets and monuments.
- **Amer:** scenic, but less convenient if most of your sightseeing is inside Jaipur.

**Practical recommendation:** Bani Park or C-Scheme is the safest choice for a 5-day first visit.

# Day 1 — Pink City & Royal Jaipur

## Morning

### 8:00 AM — Breakfast

Have a proper breakfast near your hotel.

Good local options include:

- Kachori
- Poha
- Chai
- Lassi

Do not make breakfast too heavy because the morning involves considerable walking.

### 9:00 AM — Hawa Mahal

Start at **Hawa Mahal**, one of Jaipur's most recognizable landmarks.

Spend approximately **30–60 minutes** here.

**What to do:**
- Photograph the famous façade from the street.
- Enter the monument if you want to explore its interior.
- Walk through the surrounding old-city streets.

### 10:15 AM — City Palace

Walk/drive toward **City Palace**.

Allow roughly **1.5–2 hours**.

Focus on:

- Courtyards
- Royal residences
- Decorative gates
- Museum collections
- Rajput and Mughal-influenced architecture

### 12:30 PM — Jantar Mantar

Visit **Jantar Mantar**, immediately near City Palace.

The site contains 19 astronomical instruments and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Rajasthan Tourism currently lists entry hours as **9:00 AM–7:00 PM**, with the last ticket issued at 6:30 PM.

Allow **45–75 minutes**.

## Afternoon

### 1:45 PM — Lunch

Eat around the old city / MI Road area.

Try:

- Dal baati churma
- Rajasthani thali
- Gatte ki sabzi
- Ker sangri
- Chaas

### 3:00 PM — Old City Walk

Explore:

- Johari Bazaar
- Bapu Bazaar
- Tripolia Bazaar
- Local handicraft shops

Do not rush through the markets. This is one of the best parts of Jaipur for shopping and street photography.

### 5:30 PM — Café / Rest

Return to your hotel or stop at a café.

Take a 1–2 hour break.

## Evening

### 7:00 PM — Dinner

Have a Rajasthani dinner.

**Suggested focus:** traditional thali rather than a generic North Indian restaurant.

### 9:00 PM — Optional Night Drive

If you still have energy, take a short drive through the illuminated Pink City.

**Day 1 target:** Keep this day mostly walkable and avoid unnecessary long-distance travel.

# Day 2 — Amer Fort, Jaigarh & Jal Mahal

This is the major fort day.

## Morning

### 7:30 AM — Breakfast

Eat early.

### 8:15 AM — Leave for Amer

Amer Fort is about **11 km from central Jaipur**, according to Rajasthan Tourism.

Try to reach early to avoid the worst combination of crowds, heat and traffic.

### 9:00 AM — Amer Fort

Spend approximately **2.5–3 hours**.

Prioritize:

- Jaleb Chowk
- Diwan-i-Aam
- Sheesh Mahal
- Palace courtyards
- Ganesh Pol
- Gardens
- Views over the surrounding hills

Amer Fort is a large palace complex, so do not plan another major attraction immediately afterward.

### 12:00 PM — Panna Meena ka Kund

Stop at **Panna Meena ka Kund**, the historic stepwell near Amer.

Allow around **30–45 minutes**.

## Afternoon

### 1:00 PM — Lunch

Have lunch around Amer or return toward Jaipur.

### 2:30 PM — Jaigarh Fort

Spend around **1.5–2 hours**.

Focus on:

- Fort walls
- Cannon
- Hilltop views
- Water-storage system
- Military architecture

### 4:45 PM — Jal Mahal

Drive back toward Jaipur and stop at **Jal Mahal**.

The usual plan here is a roadside/lakeside photo stop rather than a long visit.

Allow **30–45 minutes**.

## Evening

### 6:00 PM — Return to Hotel

Rest.

### 7:30 PM — Dinner

Try a rooftop restaurant or traditional Rajasthani restaurant.

### Optional

During **May–September**, Rajasthan Tourism lists the Amber Fort sound-and-light show at **7:30 PM in English and 8:30 PM in Hindi**. Check the current schedule before planning around it.

**Day 2 target:** Amer + Jaigarh are the priorities. Do not add five more monuments just because they appear close on a map.

---

# Day 3 — Nahargarh, Gaitore & Museums

## Morning

### 8:00 AM — Breakfast

### 9:00 AM — Nahargarh Fort

Spend approximately **2 hours**.

Main reasons to visit:

- Jaipur city views
- Fort architecture
- Photography
- Madhavendra Bhawan

Avoid staying too long if the weather is hot or rainy.

### 11:30 AM — Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan

Visit the royal cenotaphs at Gaitore.

Allow **45–60 minutes**.

This is quieter than the major forts and provides a different architectural experience.

## Afternoon

### 1:00 PM — Lunch

Return toward central Jaipur.

### 2:30 PM — Albert Hall Museum

Spend **1.5–2 hours**.

This works particularly well if the weather is hot or rainy because it gives you an indoor portion of the itinerary.

### 5:00 PM — Rest / Café

Take a break.

## Evening

### 6:30 PM — Birla Mandir

Visit Birla Mandir around sunset/evening.

Allow approximately **45 minutes**.

### 7:30 PM — Dinner

Have dinner around C-Scheme, MI Road or another convenient area.

**Day 3 target:** This day deliberately mixes outdoor sightseeing with indoor attractions so you do not burn out.

# Day 4 — Jaipur Culture, Food & Shopping

Day 4 is intentionally less aggressive.

## Morning

### 8:30 AM — Breakfast

### 9:30 AM — Patrika Gate

Visit Patrika Gate for photography.

Allow **30–45 minutes**.

### 10:30 AM — Local Market / Handicrafts

Depending on your interests, shop for:

- Block-print textiles
- Blue pottery
- Mojari/jutti
- Handicrafts
- Jewellery
- Rajasthani décor
- Spices

## Important Shopping Rule

Do not buy expensive jewellery, gemstones or precious items simply because a seller says they are "government approved" or "export quality."

For expensive purchases:

1. Compare multiple shops.
2. Ask for a detailed bill.
3. Check certification.
4. Avoid pressure sales.
5. Do not assume a tourist-shop recommendation means the price is fair.

## Afternoon

### 1:00 PM — Rajasthani Lunch

Make lunch the main food experience of the day.

Try:

- Dal baati churma
- Gatte
- Ker sangri
- Bajra roti
- Churma
- Chaas

### 2:30 PM — Hotel Rest

Take a proper break.

This is important. Five days of Jaipur sightseeing becomes unnecessarily tiring if every afternoon is spent outside.

## Evening

### 5:00 PM — Pink City Evening

Return to the old city.

Explore the markets at a slower pace.

### 7:30 PM — Final Shopping

Buy anything you deliberately decided to purchase earlier.

### 9:00 PM — Dinner

Make this your more relaxed dinner.

**Day 4 target:** Food + shopping + culture, not another marathon sightseeing day.

# Day 5 — Flexible Final Day

Keep Day 5 flexible because your departure time may vary.

## Option A — Relaxed Jaipur Day

Best if your train/flight is later in the evening.

### Morning

- Breakfast
- Revisit a market
- Buy souvenirs
- Café

### Afternoon

- Lunch
- Hotel checkout
- Store luggage
- Visit one remaining attraction

### Evening

Leave for Jaipur Airport / Railway Station with sufficient buffer.

---

# Option B — Abhaneri Stepwell

If you have a full day and want something outside Jaipur, consider **Abhaneri / Chand Baori**.

This is a better option if you are interested in architecture and history rather than shopping.

Plan approximately:

- 7:00 AM — Leave Jaipur
- 9:00 AM — Abhaneri
- 10:30 AM — Explore
- 12:00 PM — Return
- 2:00 PM — Lunch in Jaipur
- 3:00 PM onward — Shopping/rest
- Evening — Departure

---

# Option C — Pushkar Day Trip

Rajasthan Tourism lists **Pushkar at about 145 km from Jaipur**.

This is possible as a day trip, but it makes Day 5 significantly more travel-heavy.

Choose Pushkar only if you specifically want to see Pushkar. Do not choose it just to add another destination to your trip.

# Food You Should Try

## Must-Try Rajasthani Food

- Dal Baati Churma
- Gatte ki Sabzi
- Ker Sangri
- Bajra Roti
- Pyaz Kachori
- Mawa Kachori
- Mirchi Bada
- Ghewar
- Lassi
- Chaas

## Simple Food Strategy

Instead of eating at famous restaurants every day:

**Breakfast:** local breakfast  
**Lunch:** Rajasthani thali  
**Evening:** street food/snacks  
**Dinner:** restaurant/rooftop

This gives you more variety without making every meal expensive.

# Getting Around Jaipur

## Best Option for Comfort

**Cab + driver for full sightseeing days**

Recommended for:

- Day 2
- Day 3
- Day 5 day trip

## Auto/E-rickshaw

Useful for short distances, particularly around the old city.

Confirm the fare before starting if the ride is not metered.

## Walking

Best around:

- Hawa Mahal
- City Palace
- Jantar Mantar
- Johari Bazaar
- Bapu Bazaar

Do not attempt to walk between every attraction. Jaipur is larger than the Pink City area shown in tourist photos.

# Important Practical Tips

## 1. Start Early

For forts, early morning is much better than midday.

## 2. Do Not Overpack the Itinerary

The common mistake is trying to visit:

Amer + Jaigarh + Nahargarh + City Palace + Jantar Mantar + Albert Hall + markets + temples

all in one or two days.

You will spend too much time in traffic and too little time actually experiencing Jaipur.

## 3. Keep One Flexible Block Every Day

Rain, traffic and fatigue can easily change your schedule.

## 4. Carry Water

Especially on fort days.

## 5. Check Monument Timings Before Leaving

Official timings can change because of festivals, maintenance, weather or special events.

## 6. Keep Extra Time for Departure

Jaipur traffic can make a short-looking journey take considerably longer.
